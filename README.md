 Logistics Operations Analytics Capstone | TS Academy

 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Data Analytics training at TS Academy. The objective was to transform raw logistics operations data covering 2022–2024 into meaningful business insights that could support operational monitoring and decision-making.

The project involved analyzing 14 operational datasets covering loads, trips, drivers, trucks, customers, routes, fuel purchases, maintenance records, delivery events, safety incidents, and fleet performance metrics.

Tools and Skills

Power BI — Data visualization and dashboard development
Power Query — Data cleaning and transformation
DAX — Measures and KPI calculations
Data Modeling — Establishing relationships between datasets
Business Intelligence — Identifying trends, operational issues, and improvement opportunities

Dashboard Pages

1. Revenue Analysis

Examines billed revenue, operating profitability, revenue trends, customer contributions, and load-type performance.

2. Fleet & Operations

Evaluates fleet utilization, fuel expenditure, mileage, maintenance costs, vehicle downtime, and operational efficiency.

3. Safety & Service

Analyzes safety incidents, preventable and at-fault incidents, claims, vehicle damage, and delivery detention.

4. Business Insights

Summarizes the key findings and translates the analysis into practical business recommendations.

Key Findings

298.62M in total billed revenue was generated during the reporting period.
194.64M in operating profit was recorded, with an operating margin of 65.18%.
Fleet utilization averaged 83.04%.
The fleet covered approximately 122 million miles.
Fuel expenditure amounted to approximately 95.59M.
Maintenance costs were approximately 5.73M, while recorded fleet downtime reached approximately 72,230.5 hours.
The event-based on-time delivery rate was 44.61%, highlighting an opportunity to improve service reliability.
A total of 170 safety incidents were recorded, of which 37.65% were classified as preventable.
Total claims amounted to approximately 2.65M.

Note: Financial values are reported in the dataset's currency units. Figures are based on the Power BI measures and reporting definitions used in this project.

Business Recommendations

1. Improve delivery reliability by investigating late deliveries and recurring operational bottlenecks.
2. Strengthen preventive safety practices to reduce preventable incidents and claims.
3. Monitor fuel expenditure and fuel cost per mile to identify opportunities for cost control.
4. Improve maintenance planning and fleet availability to reduce downtime.
5. Use customer, load-type, and monthly performance trends to support operational planning.

Project Learning Outcomes

This capstone strengthened my practical understanding of data cleaning, Power Query, data modeling, DAX, dashboard design, KPI analysis, and business insight communication.

It also reinforced an important lesson: data analytics is not only about presenting numbers, but about understanding what those numbers mean and how they can inform better business decisions.
